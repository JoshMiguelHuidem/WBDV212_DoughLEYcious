## Project Description
A web-based pastry pre-order and management system built for **Dough LEYcious**. The platform allows customers to browse freshly baked cookies, cakes, and breads, customize box or per-piece orders, and submit pre-orders online, while providing administrators with a dashboard to manage products, pricing, stock levels, and incoming orders.

## Features Implemented
- **Home Page:** Showcases featured bakes, brand highlights, and quick navigation.
- **Order Options:** Supports pre-ordering per piece or in curated boxes (6, 8, or 12 pieces).
- **Menu Page:** Categorized product catalog with real-time stock status and an interactive product details modal.
- **Shopping Cart & Checkout:** Dynamic cart with quantity controls, NCR delivery validation, GCash payment reference submission, and printable order receipts.
- **About Us & Contact Pages:** Brand story, core values, founder profile, and customer inquiry section.
- **Administrative Management Panel:** Secure admin login to update product prices, toggle stock availability, hide or delete items, and track customer orders.
